3. "COMPLETE NEW API" — WHAT WE CREATE VS WHAT WE REUSE
================================================================================

  Recommended (migrate, do not duplicate):

    Keep Worker name        cyvra-mobile-api
    Keep Hyperdrive         cyvra-mobile-neon → floral-art-02749206
    Keep secrets            RESEND_*, SESSION_SECRET, then ADMIN_API_TOKEN
    Add custom domain       api.cyvoriq.co.in  (Worker custom domains)
    CORS allowlist          www / admin / accounts .cyvoriq.co.in

  Why not a second Worker on day one:
    Two Workers = two Hyperdrive bindings, two secret sets, split /health,
    split G6/G7 routes. High chance of writing evidence to the wrong place.
    A new HOSTNAME is what customers and admin see. That is the "new API".

  Optional later rename of the Worker is cosmetic. Not required for launch.

  Do not point anything at api.cyvra.co.in.
