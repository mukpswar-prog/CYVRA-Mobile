     Evidence : HostProtocolDispatcher.kt
                - SANITIZE_START L768:

6. IDENTITY ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Contract mismatch: see D-1. Two semantically distinct identities at the
     interface; one value passed at the orchestrator call site.
6.2  Canonical meaning: docs/DEVICE_EVIDENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md section 9.3
     (L370-376): deviceLifecycleId is "CYVRA's longer-lived service/registry
     identity for a device lifecycle", is "not proof of physical identity by
     itself", and "must not be derived solely from IMEI, serial, MAC address,
     or Android ID".
6.3  WHY IT CANNOT BE INVENTED: no registry, service, or propagation
     mechanism for deviceLifecycleId exists anywhere in apps/host/src/main
     (repo-wide search); the orchestration layer has no supported way to
     obtain it. Any substitute (serial, session UUID, hardware hash) either
     violates section 9.3 or collapses two distinct identity domains - the
     very defect being reported. The origin is therefore an UNRESOLVED DESIGN
     DECISION (U-1); no one-line fix is proposed.
6.4  Test-coverage gap: no test asserts the identity VALUES that reach the
     Android component; a red-first orchestrator binding test is a prerequisite
     of any D-1 fix (section 9).
